Our Story

About Urban Threads

Urban Threads began as a small local store with a simple idea — bring quality, trendy clothing to Surat at prices that feel fair. We focus on pieces you’ll actually wear: comfortable everyday wear, polished casuals, and festive outfits for the moments that matter.

Whether you’re looking for a new kurti, a reliable pair of jeans, or something special for a celebration, our team is here to help you find the right style and size. No pressure — just honest advice and a carefully curated selection.
